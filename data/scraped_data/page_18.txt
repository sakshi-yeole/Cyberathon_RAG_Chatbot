SEO and Marketing
SEO (Search Engine Optimization) and marketing are essential components of digital strategy aimed at increasing online visibility, driving traffic, and promoting products or services.
SEO focuses on optimizing websites to rank higher in search engine results. This involves keyword research, on-page optimization (like improving title tags, meta descriptions, and content quality), off-page optimization (such as link building), and technical SEO (enhancing site speed, mobile-friendliness, and crawlability).

Marketing encompasses broader strategies to reach and engage target audiences. This includes content marketing (creating valuable and relevant content), social media marketing (leveraging platforms like Facebook, Twitter, and Instagram), email marketing (sending targeted messages to subscribers), and paid advertising (using PPC campaigns on Google AdWords or social media ads).
